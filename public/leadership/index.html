Teaching Assistants

Our TAs are students, vetted by us, that are strong in teaching certain subjects. TAs help out in office hours to explain difficult concepts for students.

Since we are just starting the club, we are recruiting TAs. As a TA you will have the opportunity to teach others, earn service hours, prepare for your own MCAT, and gain leadership roles.

To become a TA, simply fill out the following google form. From there we will schedule a time for you to interview with some of the leadership team. The interview is relatively low stakes where we simply ask you to teach us some subjects from your chosen teaching interests.
